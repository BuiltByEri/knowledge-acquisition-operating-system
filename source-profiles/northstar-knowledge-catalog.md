# Northstar Knowledge Catalog Source Profile

## Source Identity

- Source name: Northstar Knowledge Catalog
- Source code: NORTHSTAR
- Source owner: Example Product Operations Team
- Source type: Mixed knowledge catalog
- Public or internal: Example-only internal catalog
- Access method: Static example files
- Update cadence: Monthly

## Source Surfaces

- Northstar Product Index
- Northstar Policy Library
- Northstar Course Directory
- Northstar Service Catalog
- Northstar Research Archive

## Included Objects

- Product records
- Policy records
- Course records
- Service records
- Research records when needed for provenance, alias, conflict, or downstream readiness context

## Excluded Objects

- Personal notes
- Draft comments
- Untagged brainstorming material
- External vendor collateral

## Known Conflicts

The Research Archive uses an older owner claim for the Data Governance Standard. The Service Catalog also contains a retired service that conflicts with the current service availability claim. Both conflicts should be preserved and resolved or withheld through governed decisions.

## Provenance Expectations

Every mapped source record should preserve the source record key, canonical entity key when applicable, relationship type, confidence, alias state, variant state, conflict state, and retrieval-exposure decision.

## Retrieval Expectations

Approved canonical entities may be exposed to downstream indexing or retrieval only when the exposure decision allows it. Non-canonical source records may be preserved for provenance without being exposed downstream.
